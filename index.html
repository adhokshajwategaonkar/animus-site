    why other apps didn't stick.

    ✕“just open the app and write it down”
    opening an app was the hard part.by the time it loaded, the thought was gone. animus lives in WhatsApp, which is already open.
    ✕“set up your folders and tags first”
    you don't organise anything.no folders, no projects, no weekly review. each thing is sorted as it arrives.
    ✕“don't break your streak!”
    taking a break costs nothing.there's no streak to lose and no wall of overdue alerts waiting for you. if something is late, only that item says so.

    start with one thing.

    it's free while it's small. sorting each message costs real money, so sign-ups are limited for now. anything you send is always saved, even when a limit is hit.
