how it works

three steps, and you only do the first one.

01

send

a link. a sentence. “book dentist friday”. whatever's about to fall out of your head — into the chat, and out of your mind.

02

sorted

claude reads it, decides what it is, titles it, dates it. links get opened and summarised. you get one line back, instantly.

03

surfaced

it lands in a feed ranked by what's going stale. done, snooze, or drop — one tap each. nothing needs filing.
