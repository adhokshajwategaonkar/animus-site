privacy

what animus keeps, and how to get rid of it.

Animus is a small, free beta run by one person. This page describes what it actually does with your data, in plain terms, rather than covering every hypothetical.

Last updated 21 August 2026.

What it collects

  • Your email address and a password, to have an account at all. The password is stored hashed, never in readable form.
  • A display name, if you give one. Optional.
  • Your phone number, once you link WhatsApp. This is what connects an incoming message to your account — it is the account key, not a marketing list.
  • The things you capture. The text of messages you send Animus on WhatsApp, or type into the app. This is the product; there is no version of it that does not store what you sent.
  • What the parse produced — a title, a type, an urgency, sometimes a date — derived from your capture.
  • Public page details for links you send, such as the title and description, fetched from the page itself so the item reads as something rather than a bare URL.
  • Settings: timezone, whether nudges are on, quiet hours, notification preferences.
  • A web push subscription, only if you turn notifications on.
  • Usage counts: how many captures, and what each one cost to process. Used to keep the beta inside a fixed budget.

No advertising, no trackers, no sale of data. There is no third-party analytics or advertising code in the site or the app, and your captures are not used to train anyone's models beyond the single processing call described below.

Who it passes through

Animus is a small app standing on other people's infrastructure. Your data touches these:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) — every WhatsApp message to or from Animus travels through Meta, under their terms and privacy policy.
  • A language model provider — the text of a capture is sent, once, to be turned into a structured item. Currently Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude), depending on configuration.
  • Supabase — the database where your account and items live.
  • Render — where the application runs.

Those providers process data on servers that may be outside your country.

How long it's kept

Items stay until you delete them or ask for the account to be removed. Archived and dropped items remain until then too — the point of the archive is that it is still there.

Deleting it

Email adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask. Everything tied to it — account, phone number, items, usage records — is deleted, and you'll get confirmation when it's done.

Deleting your Animus account does not delete the chat history sitting in your own WhatsApp app, which is on your phone and yours to clear.

Children

Animus isn't intended for anyone under 13, and accounts aren't knowingly created for them.

Security, honestly stated

Traffic is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and access to the database is restricted to the application. It is also a beta built by one person, so it makes no claim to enterprise-grade security. Don't put anything in it that would seriously hurt you to lose or expose.

Changes

If this changes materially, the date above changes and anyone with an account gets told.
